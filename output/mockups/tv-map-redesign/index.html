What changed and why

Each number matches a marker on the screen. Purple = fixes from the review. Teal = future features (turn on "Future features" to see them).

Patches

  1. 1
    No scrollbar, TV-safe edgesPATCHThe stage is a fixed 1920×1080 box with overflow: hidden. Text stays inside a 2.5% inset, so TV overscan cannot cut it off. Turn on "TV-safe area" to check this.
  2. 2
    Staff panel replaced by a real legendPATCH"32 tracked · 15 on map" is gone. The legend is built from the same route list that draws the lines, so the two cannot get out of sync.
  3. 3
    Clear "You Are Here"PATCHA large red pin with a white outline, a pulse ring and a red label. Bus bubbles fade when they pass close to it, so the pin always stays visible.
  4. 4
    Departure rows put minutes firstPATCHThe order is route → destination → minutes → platform. Minutes are 58px (readable from about 11 ft). Distance is small grey text. The operator logo column is gone. Headsigns are shortened ("Crosstown N"). A street stop has an outlined box, so nobody looks for "Platform 14".
  5. 5
    Readable bus bubblesPATCHEach bubble uses its route colour. Direction is an arrow on the edge of the bubble, not an 8px "N" or "S". Every route has its own colour. GTFS colours 8A and 8B both black, so 8B is shown in a proposed blue. GTFS 12 pink (#F8A1BE) is too pale on a light basemap, so it is deepened.
  6. 6
    Barrie Simulator basemap, real routes, rider landmarksPATCHThe basemap is a top-down Barrie Simulator render (10.8 m per pixel, no labels). Route lines are the real GTFS shapes, projected with the same maths, so they sit on the right streets. Landmark dots sit on real stop locations, including RVH, Georgian College and Park Place. The blank area at the top right and the straight edge across the bay are the city limits: the simulator has no data past them.
  7. 7
    Two columns: nothing covers the mapPATCHThe map gets 1156px and departures get 764px. The legend is a strip under the map, not a box on top of it. The old floating panels hid about 30% of the routes.
  8. 8
    Shape as well as colourPATCHGO uses a square badge and LINX uses a circle with a yellow ring. Line colours are only a second cue. This helps riders with red-green colour blindness.
  9. 9
    Quieter header, bigger clockPATCHThe title is smaller (40px, half in light weight), so it competes less with the departures. The clock and freshness chip are bigger, because riders check them.
  10. 10
    Freshness you can trustPATCHThe "Updated just now" chip turns amber when the data is stale. Turn on "Stale data": buses go grey and stop moving, and the times change to "~15 · scheduled".
  11. 11
    Static amber alert bannerPATCHThe banner does not scroll. Alerts change every 8 s, and dots show how many there are. The wording is shorter: "Thanksgiving Monday, Oct 12: No Barrie Transit service." Amber sets alerts apart from the navy header.
  12. –
    Burn-in protection and night modePATCHThe whole stage moves by up to 4px every few minutes (the demo moves every 2 s). Night dim lowers brightness after hours. Use the toolbar to try both.

  3. 14
